How the scores work

Favourite +8 · pin +4 · saved +2 · chosen category +6. Recency: within 24 hours +6, 3 days +4, 7 days +3, 30 days +1; older +0. History: floor(log₂(events + 1)), capped at +6 per creator, using the selected profile’s imported record.

After each pick, subtract 3 for each previously selected occurrence of its categories. Never repeat a creator. Ties use newest publication then video ID. Alternatives continue the same selection process. Scores are local sorting rules, not predictions of quality or how much you will enjoy a video.

Dismissed and manually marked-watched items are always excluded. History records do not prove completion or viewing duration. Missing history is unknown. No transcripts, video summaries or sensitive traits are inferred.
